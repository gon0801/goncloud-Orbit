## Lectura complementaria: la ida no puede chocar con los índices de dedupe

La ida cambia `fee_type` de NULL a `refund`. Los índices `ledger_dedupe_sin_orden` y
`ledger_dedupe_con_orden` (0001) incluyen `fee_type`; `ledger_dedupe_source` no.

```sql
select coalesce(fee_type,'<NULL>'), count(*), count(*) filter (where source_event_id is null)
from ledger_event where platform = 'meli' and kind = 'refund' group by 1 order by 1
```

```
<NULL>|62|0
```

Las 62 traen `source_event_id`: su dedupe es `ledger_dedupe_source (platform, kind,
source_event_id)`, que no mira `fee_type`. No hay ninguna devolución MeLi ya etiquetada
`refund`. Una re-ingesta antes o después de la migración (o tras la reversa) cae en el
mismo índice y no duplica.

```sql
-- pares (sin etiqueta, ya 'refund') con la misma clave natural sin source_event_id
select count(*) from ledger_event n join ledger_event e on e.platform = n.platform
  and e.kind = n.kind and e.fee_type = 'refund' and n.fee_type is null
  and n.source_event_id is null and e.source_event_id is null
  and e.order_id is not distinct from n.order_id and e.event_date = n.event_date
  and e.amount = n.amount and e.amount_currency = n.amount_currency
where n.platform = 'meli' and n.kind = 'refund'
```

```
0
```
